Roni Luokkamäki

Roni Luokkamäki is a Helsinki-based visual artist studying at Vapaa Taidekoulu (Free Art School). In his paintings he is interested in opposites, and in finding balance by reconciling them rather than through compromise. In his work he also sets out to examine clumsiness and awkwardness by pictorial means, and to find in these subjects a beauty that goes beyond the superficial.
