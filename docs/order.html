Order

Every work in the gallery is an original. To order one, use the button on the work itself, or email roni.luokkamaki@gmail.com and name the piece you want. I confirm availability, the final price and the delivery cost by email before any payment is made.

1. Payments

Payment is agreed with each order, usually by MobilePay or bank transfer. You will receive the payment details in writing once the work is reserved for you, and the piece is dispatched after payment has cleared.

2. Delivery

Works are shipped within Finland with UPS, from 15 EUR depending on the size and weight of the piece. The exact cost is confirmed with your order before you pay.

Everything is packed flat or boxed with protective wrapping. Larger pieces are quoted individually.

3. Returns

Works are made by hand, and pieces made or finished to order are exempt from the normal exchange and return right.

If a work reaches you damaged, contact me within 14 days of delivery with a photograph and we will put it right.
