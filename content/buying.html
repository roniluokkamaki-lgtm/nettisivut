Buying

Each painting in the gallery that's for sale shows its price and a Buy link. That link opens an email to roni.luokkamaki@gmail.com with the painting's name already filled in.

how it works

  • Inquiry. Email me using the Buy link, or write directly naming the painting you want.
  • Confirmation. I'll confirm the piece is still available and we agree on the details by email.
  • Pickup & payment. Pieces are picked up in person; payment is agreed per sale in the email exchange.

Not currently shipping — if you're not able to pick the piece up in person, mention it in your email and we'll see what's possible.
